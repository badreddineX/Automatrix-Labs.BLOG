Marketing teams don't have a shortage of AI tools to choose from anymore. What they have is a shortage of time to figure out which ones actually earn a place in their workflow versus which ones just add another login to manage. We've spent the past few months testing the tools marketers lean on most, and this guide breaks down what each one is genuinely good at.

This isn't a list of every AI tool with a marketing use case. It's a shorter, more useful list: the handful of tools that come up again and again when working marketers talk about what's actually in their stack this year.

**Key Takeaways**
- ChatGPT and Claude remain the default choice for marketing copywriting, research, and campaign brainstorming, usually starting free with paid tiers around $20/month.
- Jasper is worth the higher price mainly for teams that need consistent brand voice across many writers.
- Canva's AI features cover most marketers' visual needs without hiring a designer for every asset.
- AI-powered social schedulers save the most time on caption variations and optimal posting windows, not full strategy.
- Most solo marketers can run a lean, effective stack for $50-80/month total across two or three tools.

## Which AI Tool Is Best for Marketing Copywriting?

ChatGPT and Claude are the two most common starting points for marketing copywriting, and both handle the bulk of everyday writing tasks, ad copy, email drafts, landing page sections, well. According to OpenAI's own pricing page, ChatGPT offers a usable free tier alongside a $20/month Plus plan, and Anthropic prices Claude similarly.

In our experience, the two models feel different in practice even though they cover similar ground. ChatGPT tends to produce punchier, more conventional marketing copy out of the box, the kind of thing you could drop into an ad straight away. Claude tends to write longer, more considered copy that often needs less editing for tone but occasionally reads a touch more formal.

Neither tool "knows" your brand voice automatically. You get noticeably better output from both once you paste in a few examples of your best-performing copy and ask the model to match that tone, rather than starting from a blank prompt every time.

### When to Pick Jasper Instead

Jasper is built specifically for marketing teams, and its main advantage over a general chat assistant is brand voice consistency across multiple writers. Jasper's own pricing page lists plans that scale by seat and usage, generally landing higher than a single ChatGPT or Claude subscription.

If you're a solo marketer or freelancer, Jasper is usually overkill; a well-prompted ChatGPT or Claude subscription covers the same ground for less money. Jasper earns its price tag once you have three or more writers who all need to sound like the same brand without a style guide review on every draft.

## What's the Best AI Tool for Marketing Visuals?

Canva's AI features, including Magic Design and its AI image generator, cover most marketers' day-to-day visual needs without requiring a dedicated designer for every social graphic or ad creative. Canva publishes its own pricing with a functional free tier and a paid Pro plan that unlocks the fuller set of AI tools.

We tested Canva's AI image generator against a handful of real campaign brief prompts, and it consistently produced usable first drafts for social graphics and blog headers within a couple of tries. Where it struggled was highly specific brand compliance, exact logo placement or precise color-matching still needed manual cleanup afterward.

For marketers who need more advanced, photorealistic image generation beyond templated design work, a dedicated image model paired with Canva for final layout tends to produce better results than relying on Canva's generator alone for everything.

## How Do AI-Powered Social Schedulers Fit Into a Marketing Stack?

AI-powered social schedulers save marketers the most time on caption variations and posting-time optimization, not on full content strategy. Tools in this category typically layer AI caption suggestions and best-time-to-post recommendations on top of a standard scheduling calendar, priced in a similar range to Canva Pro or slightly higher depending on the number of connected accounts.

The real time savings show up when you're repurposing one piece of content across multiple platforms. Feed a scheduler an existing post, and its AI feature can spin out platform-specific caption variants, a shorter version for one channel, a slightly more casual tone for another, in a fraction of the time it takes to rewrite each one by hand.

That said, treat AI-generated captions the same way you'd treat any first draft: useful as a starting point, not something to publish unedited. We've found the suggested hashtags and CTAs in particular tend to need a human pass before going live.

## How Should You Build Your Marketing AI Stack?

Most marketers don't need five separate AI subscriptions; three tools covering copywriting, visuals, and scheduling handle nearly everything a typical campaign requires. Start with one general-purpose AI assistant (ChatGPT or Claude), add Canva for visuals, and layer in a scheduler once you're publishing consistently enough to need one.

Solo marketers and freelancers can generally run this stack for somewhere in the $50-80/month range once you account for a paid copywriting tier and Canva Pro, based on each tool's own published pricing pages. Larger teams will spend more, mainly because tools like Jasper price by seat rather than by individual usage.

If you already automate other parts of your workflow, this stack fits naturally alongside a broader automation setup. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I need both ChatGPT and Jasper?**
A: Usually not. Jasper's main advantage is brand-voice consistency across multiple writers, so solo marketers rarely need both. Pick ChatGPT or Claude first, and only add Jasper once you're managing a team of writers.

**Q: Is Canva's AI good enough to replace a designer?**
A: For routine social graphics and blog headers, often yes. For highly specific brand campaigns or photorealistic imagery, it still benefits from a designer's final review or a more specialized image tool.

**Q: What's the cheapest AI marketing stack that actually works?**
A: A free-tier chat assistant paired with Canva's free plan can cover basic needs. Most marketers see the biggest quality jump once they upgrade at least the copywriting tool to a paid tier.

**Q: Can AI tools write an entire marketing campaign on their own?**
A: Not reliably. They're strong at drafting individual assets, ad copy, captions, graphics, once you've set the strategy and offer. Campaign strategy, targeting decisions, and budget calls still need a human marketer driving them.

**Q: How often should marketers re-evaluate their AI tool stack?**
A: Every few months is reasonable given how fast this space moves. Set a recurring reminder to check whether a newer tool has meaningfully overtaken something already in your stack before renewing a subscription automatically.
