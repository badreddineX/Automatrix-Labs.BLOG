# Affiliate + Store Split Strategy (2026-08-10)

## The decision (updated 2026-08-10)

**Applies identically to both Canada (smallspacehome.ca) and UK (britishhomeinterior.co.uk).**

1. **Blog articles get a small combined notice/box** containing both affiliate
   product link(s) (Amazon Associates, Etsy affiliate) AND the user's own Fourthwall
   product(s), placed together — not two separate uncoordinated links scattered
   through the post. Include the required disclosure line in the same box (FTC
   wording for Canada/US audience, ASA wording for UK audience).
2. **Fourthwall store also gets pushed independently via social media** (Pinterest/
   Instagram pins linking straight to the store) — this continues alongside the
   in-article notice box, it isn't replaced by it.
3. **No new digital product gets built speculatively** — see Product policy below.
   This supersedes the "build the budget/life-planner template next" recommendation
   in `digital prod/STRATEGY.md`.

Why a combined notice instead of fully separate lanes: it's simpler to implement as
one small on-page component, and pairs a proven/reviewed affiliate item with the
user's own product in the same trust-building moment. Tradeoff accepted: harder to
cleanly attribute which item in the box drove a given click/sale early on — track via
distinct affiliate link tags vs. a distinct Fourthwall UTM so they can still be told
apart in analytics even though they're displayed together.

## Lane 1 — Affiliate links on articles

**Setup (do first, before any content work):**
- Amazon Associates application (Canada + UK have separate programs — apply to both:
  Amazon.ca Associates and Amazon.co.uk Associates)
- Etsy affiliate program (via Awin or Etsy's own affiliate hub) as a secondary option
  for items that read as more "curated/handmade" than an Amazon listing would

**Placement rules:**
- Match the product to the specific article topic — not a generic "shop our
  favorites" widget. A post about renter-friendly storage links to actual storage bins,
  not an unrelated home-decor category.
- One clear in-article product mention near the top third of the post (where the
  relevant advice is) plus one at the bottom (recap/CTA) — not a sidebar-only link,
  which the current CRO audit confirmed gets ignored.
- Disclose affiliate relationship per FTC/ASA requirements (a short "may earn a
  commission" line) — required, not optional, in both US and UK jurisdictions.

**Rollout order:** start with the 5-10 highest-traffic posts per blog (check GSC), not
all 76+ posts at once — get real click data before scaling to the full catalog.

**Tracking:** use Amazon's/Etsy's native affiliate link tagging (built-in click/sale
tracking) — no custom analytics needed to start.

## Lane 2 — Fourthwall store via social media

**Traffic source:** Pinterest and Instagram posts/pins link directly to the Fourthwall
store or a specific product page — not routed through the blog. This keeps blog CRO
(Lane 1) and store CRO (Lane 2) cleanly separated so results from each are easy to read.

**Product policy going forward:** no new product gets built speculatively. A product
only gets built after a real demand signal is found:
- Recurring GSC queries content can't fully answer (e.g. "printable small bathroom
  layout") — a real search asking for a tool, not just info
- Outsized Pinterest saves/comments on a pin about a specific system/template
- Repeated forum/Reddit requests for a specific tool in the niche

This replaces the earlier "pick a Tier 1 niche and build it" plan in
`digital prod/STRATEGY.md` — that document's research stays as reference for *what*
tends to sell if/when a signal appears, but the default action is now "wait for
evidence," not "build on spec."

## What NOT to do

- Don't copy or resell someone else's product/listing as your own — that's
  infringement, not affiliate marketing. Affiliate links must point to the real
  seller's own listing.
- Don't mix the two lanes — no affiliate links inside social pushes meant to drive
  store traffic, and no Fourthwall store links inside articles for now (keeps
  attribution clean while both lanes are being tested).
- Don't build a new digital product until a specific signal (above) justifies it.

2. Pick 5-10 highest-traffic posts per blog from GSC; match each to a real,
   specific, already-proven product.
3. Add one mid-article + one bottom-of-article affiliate mention per chosen post,

4. Increase Pinterest/Instagram cadence pointed directly at the Fourthwall store

5. Start logging GSC/Pinterest signals that would justify building a specific new
