# United Kingdom — Pinterest Country Study

## User Base
- **Monthly active users**: 15.5 million (2025)
- **Annual active users**: 14.68 million (October 2025)
- **% of British population**: 22%
- **% of UK consumers on Pinterest**: 30%
- **Global ranking**: 6th largest Pinterest market in the world
- **Platform share**: 3.23% of all social media web visits in the UK

---

## Demographics

### Gender
- Female: **71%**
- Male: **22.8%**
- Strongest female skew of any major European market

### Age Groups
- **18–24**: 28.9% of UK Pinterest users
- **25–34**: 26.8% — core purchase-decision demographic
- Gen Z comprises 42% of global users — heavily represented in UK

### Purchase Behavior
- **61%** of UK Pinterest users have made a purchase based on content seen on the platform
- UK Pinners spend **80% more monthly** than shoppers on other platforms
- Highest purchase intent of any European Pinterest market

## Targeting the UK on Pinterest

### Language
- Use **British English** spelling: colour, organise, favourite, cosy, mum, flat, garden, biscuit
- Avoid American terms: apartment (use flat), chips (use crisps), vacation (use holiday)
- UK-specific vocabulary signals to the algorithm that your content is for British users

### Peak Pinning Hours (GMT)
- Weekdays: **8PM – 11PM GMT**
- Weekends: **2PM – 9PM GMT**

## Why the UK Is a Winning Country

- 61% of users have bought based on Pinterest content — highest conversion in Europe
- Spend 80% more monthly than users on other platforms — premium audience
- 30% consumer penetration — strong reach potential
- British English content stands out — most Pinterest content is American, giving UK-specific content lower competition
- Strong affiliate market: ASOS, Marks & Spencer, John Lewis, Amazon UK, IKEA UK
- Growing platform — consistent year-on-year user growth
