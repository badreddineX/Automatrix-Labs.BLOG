# Canada — Pinterest Country Study

## User Base
- **Total Pinterest users**: 12.14 million (2025)
- **% of Canadian internet users**: 33.5%
- **Platform ranking in Canada**: 5th most popular social media platform
- **ARPU (US + Canada combined)**: $6.54 USD per user (Q1 2025) — highest in the world

---

## Demographics

### Gender
- Female users: **~70%**
- Male users: growing — **+40% YoY growth** among men

### Age Groups
- **18–24**: Highest adoption rate (54% of this age group use Pinterest)
- **25–34**: 50% penetration — core decision-making demographic
- These two groups drive the majority of Canadian Pinterest traffic

### Behavior
- **80%** of Canadian users have discovered a new brand on Pinterest
- **85%+** of Canadian Pinterest users also use Instagram
- Strong purchase intent — users come to plan and buy, not just browse

## Targeting Canada on Pinterest

### Language
- Use **US English** — Canadian audience fully accepts American spelling and terminology
- Avoid UK-specific terms (flat, colour, organise) — they signal non-Canadian content
- No need for French unless specifically targeting Quebec

### Peak Pinning Hours (Eastern Time)
- Weekdays: **8PM – 11PM EST**
- Weekends: **2PM – 9PM EST**

### Key Canadian Holidays to Target
| Holiday | Date | Content Opportunity |
|---------|------|-------------------|
| Canadian Thanksgiving | 2nd Monday October | Food, home decor, family |
| Victoria Day | 3rd Monday May | Outdoor, garden, BBQ |
| Canada Day | July 1st | Patriotic, summer |
| Boxing Day | December 26th | Shopping deals, home |
| Back to School | Late August | Organization, decor |

### Keywords to Use
- Use the same keywords as the US market — Canadian users search in American English
- Avoid Canadian slang in board titles — keep board names universally searchable

---

## Why Canada Is a Winning Country

- Nearly same monetization value as the US (shared ARPU pool)
- Smaller competition than US — easier to rank organically
- English-speaking — zero language barrier for US-style content
- High purchasing power — strong affiliate commission rates
- 33.5% internet user penetration — high reach potential
