# Subniche Selection by Country

---

## Canada

| Priority | Subniche |
|----------|----------|
| #1 | Small Space Home Organization & Apartment Decor |
| #2 | Meal Prep & Quick Weeknight Dinners |
| #3 | Budgeting for Beginners & Side Hustles |

**Focus on**: Small Space Home Organization & Apartment Decor
- Urban Canada (Toronto, Vancouver, Montreal) = apartment living is the norm
- Evergreen content, high purchase intent, less competition than broad home decor
- Strong affiliate opportunities: IKEA, Amazon CA, Wayfair Canada

## United Kingdom

| Priority | Subniche |
|----------|----------|
| #1 | British Home Interior Styling & Room Makeovers |
| #2 | Budget Meal Planning & Easy British Dinners |
| #3 | Capsule Wardrobe & Affordable UK Fashion |

**Focus on**: British Home Interior Styling & Room Makeovers
- Most Pinterest home decor is American — UK content faces far less competition
- Distinct British aesthetics (cottagecore, maximalist, Victorian terrace) are underserved
- UK users spend 80% more monthly than users on other platforms
- Strong affiliate opportunities: IKEA UK, John Lewis, Dunelm, Wayfair UK
