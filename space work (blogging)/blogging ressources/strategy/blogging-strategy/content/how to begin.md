---

## The Big Picture

You are building **3 pilot blogs** — one per country, one niche each.

| Blog | Country | Niche | Pinterest Account |
|------|---------|-------|-------------------|
| Blog 1 | Canada | Small Space Home Organization & Apartment Decor | @[yourbrand]ca |
| Blog 2 | United Kingdom | British Home Interior Styling & Room Makeovers | @[yourbrand]uk |
| Blog 3 | Australia | Outdoor & Coastal Home Decor + Entertaining | @[yourbrand]au |

Each blog is independent — its own domain, its own Pinterest account, its own audience.
Once these 3 work, you scale to 9 (add 2nd and 3rd niche per country).

**Revenue model**: Affiliate links → Display ads → Digital products (in that order)

> Use country-specific TLDs (.ca, .co.uk, .com.au) — this alone boosts country-targeting on both Google and Pinterest.

---

### Step 2 — Set Up Each Blog (WordPress or similar)

For each of the 3 blogs:
- Install WordPress (or use a builder like Kadence, GeneratePress theme)
- Set language to match country (en-CA, en-GB, en-AU)
- Set timezone to country capital (Toronto, London, Sydney)
- Install: Rank Math SEO, WP Rocket (speed), ShortPixel (image compression)
- Make sure site is mobile-first (single column, large text, white space)

> Reference: `content/wining blog format.md` → Mobile-First Rules section

---

### Step 3 — Create 3 Pinterest Business Accounts

One Pinterest account per blog. Do NOT mix content across accounts.

**For each account:**
1. Switch to Business Account (free)
2. Set country in settings to match your target (Canada / United Kingdom / Australia)
3. Set language: English (Canada) / English (UK) / English (Australia)
4. Claim your blog domain inside Pinterest settings
5. Enable Rich Pins (auto-pulls title + description from your blog posts)

**Profile setup:**
- Username: keyword-rich (e.g. "SmallSpaceHomeCa" not "myblog2024")
- Bio: 160 characters, include 2–3 target keywords + country signal
- Profile photo: simple logo or clean lifestyle image matching your niche

> Reference: `pinterest analyze/wining country/targeting audience.md` → Full targeting guide

### Step 6 — Write Every Post Using the Same Structure

Every single blog post follows the 14-element structure from the blog format file.

**Quick checklist for every post:**
- [ ] Title tag under 60 characters — keyword first
- [ ] TL;DR summary at the TOP (2–4 bullets) — not the bottom
- [ ] Table of contents
- [ ] Short intro (2–3 sentences only)
- [ ] H2/H3 headings written as questions
- [ ] Original images or custom graphics (not stock photos)
- [ ] FAQ section with 3–7 questions — this alone boosts AI citations by 340%
- [ ] One clear CTA per post
- [ ] Internal links to 2–3 related posts
- [ ] Meta description written as ad copy (140–160 chars)
- [ ] Descriptive URL slug (no dates)

**Word count targets:**
- Listicle: 1,500–3,000 words
- How-to guide: 1,500–2,500 words
- Ultimate guide: 2,800–3,400 words

> Reference: `content/wining blog format.md` → Full 14-element structure

### Step 7 — Language Rules Per Country

**Canada:**
- Standard American/Canadian English is fine
- Add: "in Canada," "Canadian apartments," local price references in CAD ($)
- Reference Canadian retailers: IKEA Canada, Amazon.ca, Wayfair Canada, HomeSense

**UK — CRITICAL:**
- Use British English spelling throughout every post and every pin
- colour (not color), cosy (not cozy), organise (not organize), flat (not apartment), mum (not mom)
- Price in £, reference UK retailers: IKEA UK, Dunelm, John Lewis, Next Home, Wayfair UK
- Write "UK" in titles — "living room ideas UK" not "living room ideas"

**Australia:**
- Use Australian English: colour, organise, favourite (same as UK)
- Mention Australian seasons correctly (December = summer, June = winter)
- Reference Aussie retailers: Temple & Webster, Adairs, Bunnings, IKEA AU
- Add "Australia" signal in titles where possible

> Reference: `pinterest analyze/wining country/targeting audience.md`

## PHASE 3 — Pinterest Pinning Strategy (Start Week 3)

### Step 8 — Create Pins for Every Blog Post

For each blog post you publish, create **3–5 pin variations** (different image, same link).

**Pin design rules (applies to all 3 accounts):**
- Vertical format only: 1000 × 1500px (2:3 ratio)
- Text overlay at top or bottom — never center
- Hook words on image: "Ideas," "Budget," "Easy," "Before & After," "How To"
- Use real-looking photos — not generic stock images
- Keep brand colors consistent across all pins on that account

**Pin title rules:**
- Primary keyword in the first 5 words
- Example: "Small Apartment Organization Ideas on a Budget"

**Pin description rules:**
- 250–500 characters
- Include keyword 2–3 times naturally
- End with CTA: "Save this for later" or "Click to see all ideas"
- For UK: use British English spelling throughout

> Reference: `pinterest analyze/competitor behavior.md` → Pin Design & Pin Title sections

---

### Step 9 — Posting Schedule

**Daily posting target per account: 1–3 fresh pins**

Do not repin old content until you have 30+ original pins. Fresh content first.

**Best times to post (from country studies):**

| Country | Best Days | Best Times |
|---------|-----------|------------|
| Canada | Sat, Sun, Tue | 8–11pm ET |
| UK | Sat, Sun, Mon | 8–11pm GMT |
| Australia | Sat, Sun, Fri | 8–11pm AEDT |

Use Tailwind or Pinterest's own scheduler to batch-schedule pins in advance.

> Reference: country study files for peak hour detail

Plan seasonal content 6–8 weeks ahead — Pinterest is a search engine, not a social feed.

## PHASE 4 — Monetization (Month 2 onwards)

### Step 11 — Set Up Affiliate Programs

Sign up for these programs **before** you publish — so you can add affiliate links from post 1.

**Canada blog:**
- Amazon Associates Canada (amazon.ca/associates)
- Wayfair Affiliate Program
- IKEA does not have a public affiliate program — use alternatives like Amazon CA for IKEA-style products

**UK blog:**
- Amazon Associates UK (affiliate-program.amazon.co.uk)
- Awin network → Dunelm, John Lewis, Next, Wayfair UK all on Awin
- ASOS Affiliate (via Awin)

**Australia blog:**
- Amazon Associates AU (affiliate-program.amazon.com.au)
- Commission Factory (AU affiliate network) → Temple & Webster, Adairs, Bunnings
- eBay Partner Network AU

**How to use affiliate links in posts:**
- Recommend 3–5 specific products per post
- Link the product name naturally in the text — not "click here"
- Add a short disclosure at the top of every post (legal requirement in all 3 countries)

---

### Step 12 — Display Ads (When You Hit Traffic)

Once any blog reaches **50,000 sessions/month** → apply to Mediavine (best RPM)
Before 50k sessions → use Google AdSense as a placeholder (lower RPM but no minimum)

Priority: get to 50k sessions on your strongest blog first, then monetize with ads, then reinvest into the others.
