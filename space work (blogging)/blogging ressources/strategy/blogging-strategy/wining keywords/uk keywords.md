## Keyword Strategy for the UK

- Use **British English** spelling in all pins — colour, organise, cosy, favourite, flat, mum
- This alone reduces competition significantly since most Pinterest content uses American English
- Add UK-specific signals: "UK," "British," price in £ where relevant
- Target long-tail first — UK market is smaller than US, easier to rank
- Use Pinterest Trends filtered to **United Kingdom** to validate demand before creating
