## Keyword Strategy for Canada

- Use **American English spelling** — Canadian audience searches in US English
- Add action words: "ideas," "tips," "how to," "for beginners," "on a budget"
- Target long-tail keywords first — lower competition, easier to rank
- Use Pinterest Trends tool filtered to **Canada** to validate before creating content
