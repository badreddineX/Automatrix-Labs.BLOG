# Real Business — Working Notes

Started 2026-07-30, continued from a conversation in the SPACE WORK repo. Separate venture,
not tied to the SmallSpaceHome/British Home Interior blogs, but kept inside the SPACE WORK
folder (`SPACE WORK/real business/`) per user preference rather than at Desktop level —
parking the thread here to pick back up later.

## Why this exists

User wants a second business, separate from the daily-content blog grind (SPACE WORK),
so time can split across both without the blogs eating all of it. Explicitly not another
content blog.

## Where things landed (not finalized — pick back up from here)

- **Domain in play:** `outdoorcoastalhome.com` (currently the paused Australia blog under
  SPACE WORK — `blogs/australia-subn-1/website/`). Has some existing content/Pinterest
  pins from the paused blog attempt, not verified for real traffic/backlink value yet.
- **Ruled out:** a real e-commerce/dropship/POD store — user flagged this as "hard to
  handle," i.e. too much ongoing ops (inventory, fulfillment, customer service).
- **Business model direction, clarified late in the conversation:** user wants to be the
  **third party / interface** — using *other people's* products or services and earning a
  **commission**, not creating and selling their own product. This rules out the earlier
  "digital plans store" and "AI visualizer" ideas (both require building/owning a product)
  and points toward:
  1. **Service lead-gen / referral commission** (Angi/HomeAdvisor model) — e.g., a free
     tool (cost calculator) that captures a lead, sold/referred to a local contractor for
     a fee or % commission. Zero product creation, zero inventory. Flagged as the cleaner
     fit for the "pure interface" requirement.
  2. **Product affiliate** (Wirecutter model) — buying-guide/comparison pages linking to
     retailers' products (Amazon Associates, Home Depot/Lowe's, brand affiliate programs),
     earning % per sale. Caveat: needs some real content pages (not daily blogging, but
     not zero writing either) — partial fit against the "no blogging" constraint.
- **Niche suggestion on the table:** decking (deck building/deck plans/deck cost). Reasoning:
  high, proven search demand ("deck cost calculator," "deck plans," "deck design"), specific
  enough to not be generic/saturated like "outdoor living" broadly, and works with either
  lead-gen or product-affiliate models. Not yet confirmed by user — was reacting to it when
  the thread paused. Runner-up niches raised: outdoor kitchens, pools/hot tubs.
- **Assets user has, general capability (not yet confirmed as built for this specific idea):**
  Stripe account experience, Pinterest account experience, familiarity with running a
  platform — but explicitly **no Pinterest account exists yet for this specific venture**,
  and niche/model are not locked in.
