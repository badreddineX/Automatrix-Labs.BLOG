## Strategy: automate-then-expand

Vision: get one income domain automated/self-sustaining, then move to the next domain,
repeat. Agreed direction, with one refinement discussed with Claude: prove a domain
actually earns real, repeatable income before calling it "automated" and expanding —

CAD/UK is closer to first real revenue, push that one hard until it's earning
something repeatable, THEN set up the next front (Fourthwall stores, Australia,
Prosperity Press) using what worked as a playbook — not launch everything in parallel.
