IKEA designs for small spaces better than almost any other furniture brand. Most of their pieces were developed for European apartments — which are, on average, significantly smaller than North American ones. For Canadian renters trying to maximize a 500–650 sq ft apartment, this is a real advantage.

I have used IKEA products throughout my 510 sq ft Toronto apartment since I moved in three years ago. Some work exactly as designed. Some work much better when used differently than intended.

Here are 15 hacks and uses that have actually made a difference.

## 1. KALLAX as a Room Divider

The KALLAX storage unit is designed to go against a wall. Placed perpendicular to the wall — behind a sofa or across the middle of a studio apartment — it becomes a room divider that also provides storage and display space.

This is the most effective way to visually separate a sleeping zone from a living zone in a studio without building a wall.

**Products:** KALLAX 5-unit horizontal (~$139 CAD) or KALLAX 2×2 (~$79 CAD) stacked. Add DRONA fabric inserts (~$6 each) for concealed storage on the "bedroom" side.

## 2. KALLAX + Cushion = Window Bench With Storage

Place a KALLAX unit horizontally under a window at bench height, add a custom-cut foam pad with a cover on top, and you have a window bench with storage inside each cube.

The cubes hold blankets, books, board games, and seasonal items. The bench becomes a reading spot, additional seating, or just a good-looking focal point.

**Products:** KALLAX 4-cube horizontal (~$99 CAD) + bench cushion or cut foam covered in fabric (~$30–$50 CAD total)

## 3. BILLY Bookcase to the Ceiling

The standard IKEA BILLY bookcase is 202 cm tall. If your ceiling is 240–270 cm, you can add BILLY height extensions (~$40 CAD each) or OXBERG doors on top to bring the unit nearly to the ceiling.

A floor-to-ceiling bookcase takes the same floor space as a standard one but provides almost twice the storage.

This also makes the room feel taller — vertical lines draw the eye upward.

**Products:** BILLY bookcase from ~$79 CAD + BILLY height extension ~$40 CAD + OXBERG doors ~$60–$80 CAD per pair

## 4. NORDLI Bed as a Dresser Replacement

IKEA NORDLI is a platform bed with integrated drawers in the base. Depending on the configuration, it has 2–8 drawers. Used with a full or queen mattress, it replaces an entire dresser while adding zero floor footprint.

This is one of the most practical IKEA hacks for a small bedroom: instead of a bed + dresser taking up two spots of floor space, you have just a bed that happens to contain all your folded clothes.

**Products:** IKEA NORDLI bed frame with drawers from ~$399 CAD

## 5. PAX Wardrobe System for Closet-Less Bedrooms

Many older Toronto and Vancouver apartments have undersized closets or none at all. IKEA PAX wardrobe columns are completely configurable — choose the external frame, then choose what goes inside (drawers, hanging rods, shoe shelves, pull-out units).

Two PAX columns side by side create a complete wardrobe wall. Add sliding doors to keep it sleek and space-efficient.

**Products:** PAX frame from ~$179 CAD per column + interior fittings + AULI or MEHAMN sliding doors

![IKEA PAX wardrobe system filling a wall in a small bedroom, replacing a closet](/images/blog-12-img2.jpg)

## 6. SKADIS Pegboard Above the Desk

IKEA SKADIS pegboards are designed to organize an office or craft space. Mounted above the desk on the wall, they hold monitors, charging cables, small shelves, and containers for office supplies — everything that would otherwise clutter the desk surface.

A clear desk is more productive and looks significantly better in a small apartment.

**Products:** SKADIS pegboard 56 × 56 cm (~$25 CAD) + hooks and accessories (~$3–$10 each). Mount with Command large picture strips for renter-friendly installation.

## 7. MICKE Desk for the Narrowest Spaces

The IKEA MICKE desk is 73 cm wide and 50 cm deep — small enough to fit in a hallway nook, a bedroom corner, or beside a kitchen counter. It has a built-in cable management hole and a rear storage compartment.

In a studio or one-bedroom apartment, MICKE creates a proper home office zone in under 1 sq metre of floor space.

**Products:** IKEA MICKE desk from ~$99 CAD in white or black

## 8. SKUBB Under-Bed Storage Bags

SKUBB under-bed bags are flat, soft-sided bags that slide under any bed with clearance. They come in two sizes and work for seasonal clothing, extra bedding, and spare towels.

If your bed frame has no storage and you don't want to buy a new one, pair SKUBB bags with bed risers (~$25 CAD at Amazon.ca) for enough clearance.

**Products:** IKEA SKUBB under-bed bags ~$14 CAD for a 2-pack

## 9. HEMNES Nightstand as Entry Console

The IKEA HEMNES nightstand is narrow (~50 cm wide) and has two drawers — enough for keys, mail, and small accessories. It works better than many purpose-built entryway consoles and costs significantly less.

In a narrow apartment hallway, HEMNES fits where a full console table cannot.

**Products:** IKEA HEMNES 2-drawer nightstand ~$129–$179 CAD

## 10. FEJKA Fake Plants for Difficult Spots

Real plants need light and water. IKEA FEJKA artificial plants look convincingly real and can go in spots where nothing real would survive — dark corners, the top of tall cabinets, or windowless bathrooms.

In a small apartment, adding greenery in every corner creates a lush feel without the maintenance. Use FEJKA for the hard spots and real plants near windows.

**Products:** IKEA FEJKA artificial plants from ~$10–$20 CAD per plant

![Small apartment with both real and IKEA FEJKA artificial plants adding green throughout](/images/blog-12-img3.jpg)

## 11. IVAR Shelving System for Customizable Storage

IVAR is IKEA's untreated pine shelving system — modular, adjustable, and available in multiple widths. It is less well-known than KALLAX or BILLY but more flexible, and it can be painted or stained to match any interior.

Use it in storage rooms, closets, or as a freestanding wall unit in any room.

**Products:** IVAR frame + shelves from ~$50–$120 CAD depending on configuration

## 12. LACK Floating Shelves Stacked Vertically

IKEA LACK shelves are $15–$20 CAD each. Mounted in a vertical stack with 25–30 cm between shelves, they create a display and storage column on any blank wall — no floor footprint.

Use Command Picture Strips (large) for renter-safe installation. Each pair holds 7.5 kg — enough for books, plants, and decorative objects.

**Products:** IKEA LACK shelves ~$15 CAD each + Command large picture strips ~$12–$15 CAD

## 13. KORKEN Jars for Pantry and Bathroom Organization

IKEA KORKEN glass jars with lids are designed for food storage. In the pantry, they hold dry goods like pasta, rice, oats, and spices in a consistent, attractive way that makes the cabinet look curated rather than cluttered.

In the bathroom, the same jars hold cotton balls, q-tips, hair ties, and small accessories.

**Products:** IKEA KORKEN jar with lid from ~$3–$8 CAD

## 14. LILL Sheers for Every Window

IKEA LILL sheer curtain panels are $10 CAD per pair. In a small apartment where you cannot change the walls, window treatments make a significant difference to how a room looks and feels.

Sheer panels let natural light through while adding the finishing touch that makes a room look intentional. Hang them from ceiling to floor for maximum visual impact.

**Products:** IKEA LILL sheer curtains ~$10 CAD per pair

## 15. NORBERG Drop-Leaf Table for Dining Without Space

The IKEA NORBERG wall-mounted drop-leaf table folds completely flat against the wall when not in use. Extended, it seats two people. It is the perfect dining solution for a studio or one-bedroom apartment where a permanent dining table would take too much space.

Mount it on the wall at standard dining table height. When folded, it takes less than 8 cm of wall depth.

**Products:** IKEA NORBERG wall-mounted drop-leaf table ~$59 CAD

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What are the best IKEA products for small apartments in Canada?**
NORDLI bed with storage drawers, KALLAX storage unit, BILLY bookcase extended to ceiling height, MICKE compact desk, and PAX wardrobe system. These five products solve the most common small apartment problems: storage, closet space, and workspace.

**How do I use IKEA KALLAX in a small apartment?**
Horizontally as a bench under a window, vertically as a tall storage unit, or perpendicular to the wall as a room divider in a studio. Add DRONA or KUGGIS fabric inserts for concealed storage. Add drawers or doors for a more finished look. KALLAX is the most versatile small apartment furniture piece IKEA makes.

**Is IKEA good for small spaces in Canada?**
Yes — IKEA designs many products specifically for small European apartments, which translates well to Canadian rental apartments. Their furniture tends to be apartment-scaled, modular, and designed to do more than one job. IKEA Canada has locations in most major cities including Toronto, Vancouver, Calgary, and Montreal.

**How do I make my IKEA furniture look custom in a small apartment?**
Paint BILLY bookshelves in a contrasting colour and add OXBERG glass doors. Apply peel-and-stick wallpaper inside KALLAX cubes before inserting fabric inserts. Replace standard IKEA hardware with brass or matte black pulls. Add crown molding between a floor-to-ceiling BILLY and the ceiling. These changes cost $30–$80 CAD and make IKEA pieces look intentional and custom.
