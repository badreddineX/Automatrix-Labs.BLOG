The bedroom in a small apartment is asked to do a lot. It needs to be a sleeping space, a dressing room, a storage area for clothes and extra linens, and ideally a place where you can actually relax — all in a room that is typically 80 to 140 square feet.

Getting bedroom storage right in a small apartment is mostly about finding the spaces you are not using — under the bed, behind doors, above eye level — and making them work.

## 1. The Storage Bed Frame: The Biggest Single Upgrade

If you are buying a new bed, choose one with integrated storage drawers. The IKEA NORDLI platform bed with drawers starts at ~$399 CAD and adds 2–8 drawers depending on configuration.

These drawers replace a dresser entirely — folded clothes, extra bedding, towels, and seasonal items all go underneath the mattress you sleep on. The floor space a dresser would have occupied stays open.

This is the highest-impact bedroom storage decision you can make in a small apartment.

## 2. Bed Risers + SKUBB Bags: The Budget Under-Bed System

If you already have a bed without storage, bed risers ($25 CAD for a set of 4 at Amazon.ca) add approximately 15 cm of clearance. IKEA SKUBB under-bed storage bags ($14 CAD for a 2-pack) then slide underneath.

Use SKUBB bags for:
- Off-season clothing
- Extra duvet covers and pillow cases
- Spare towels and linens
- Shoes that do not fit in the closet

Total cost: ~$39 CAD for the equivalent of a full dresser drawer of hidden storage.

## 3. Slim Velvet Hangers Throughout the Closet

Standard plastic hangers are roughly 3× thicker than slim velvet hangers. In a 100 cm closet rod with 30 garments, switching to slim hangers frees approximately 30 cm of space — room for 8–10 additional items.

~$18 CAD for a 50-pack on Amazon.ca. One of the best-dollar-for-dollar improvements in the bedroom.

## 4. Second Tension Rod in the Closet

A second tension rod placed below the existing closet rod doubles the hanging space for shorter garments — shirts, folded pants, jackets, dresses. The existing rod rises to handle longer items (full-length coats and dresses).

~$8–$12 CAD at Canadian Tire or Dollar stores.

## 5. Over-Door Organizer on the Closet Door

The inside of the closet door is a frequently missed storage opportunity. An over-door organizer adds 8–24 pockets for shoes, bags, scarves, belts, and accessories.

For a small bedroom closet, this can move a significant portion of what's piled on the closet floor or shelf onto the door.

~$20–$35 CAD at Amazon.ca or Canadian Tire.

## 6. Floating Wall Shelf as a Nightstand

A conventional nightstand takes up floor space. A floating shelf at nightstand height — mounted on the wall with Command strips — provides the same surface with zero floor footprint.

IKEA LACK shelf: ~$15 CAD. Command Large Picture Strips: ~$12–$15 CAD. Total: ~$27–$30 CAD.

The shelf holds: lamp, phone and charger, water glass, and one book. Everything you actually need at bedside.

Pair with a small wall-mounted clip light (mounted with Command) to eliminate the lamp and further free up the shelf.

## 7. IKEA PAX System for Closet-Less Bedrooms

If your bedroom has no closet or an extremely small one, IKEA PAX wardrobe columns give you a configurable wardrobe wall.

Start with one column (~$179 CAD for the frame), add your interior fittings (drawers, hanging rods, shelves), and add sliding doors to keep the footprint narrow. Two columns side by side create a complete wardrobe system.

This is the most expensive option on this list but the most transformative if you have no closet.

## 8. Tall Narrow Dresser Instead of Wide Low One

If you need a dresser, choose tall and narrow over wide and low. A 5-drawer tall dresser takes the same floor space as a 2-drawer wide one — but holds 2.5× as much.

Also: a tall dresser can have a floating shelf above it, further increasing vertical storage in the same wall space.

IKEA HEMNES 6-drawer tall dresser: ~$299–$389 CAD.

## 9. Use the Space Above the Closet

Most closets have space between the top closet shelf and the ceiling. This space — often 30–60 cm — is ideal for items accessed seasonally: luggage, holiday decorations, spare bedding.

**Options:**
- Stacked clear lidded bins visible from below
- A storage bench cushion if the top shelf is accessible for occasional use
- IKEA SKUBB storage cases for luggage (~$20–$35 CAD each)

## 10. Hooks on the Back of the Bedroom Door

The back of the bedroom door — not the closet door, the bedroom entry door — holds hooks for next-day outfits, robes, handbags, and scarves. This keeps the chair in the corner from becoming a permanent clothes pile.

Command large hooks: ~$8–$12 CAD for a 4-pack. Install three across the upper third of the door.

## 11. Bench at the Foot of the Bed With Storage

If space allows, a storage bench at the foot of the bed provides:
- Seating for putting on shoes
- Storage inside (blankets, seasonal items)
- Visual anchoring for the bed

In a small bedroom, size matters: look for a bench 90–120 cm wide. Ottoman-style with a hinged lid provides easy access.

~$120–$250 CAD at Wayfair Canada or Amazon.ca.

![Storage bench at the foot of a bed in a small apartment bedroom with lid open showing stored blankets](/images/blog-18-img3.jpg)

## 12. Clear Shoe Boxes on the Closet Shelf

Shoe boxes stacked in a closet are nearly impossible to navigate — you cannot see what is inside without opening each one. Clear shoe boxes with lids let you see and access every pair at a glance.

~$5–$8 CAD per box at Amazon.ca or The Container Store. A stack of 8–10 clear boxes organized on the closet shelf is much more efficient than random shoe storage.

## 13. SKUBB Shelf Dividers to Prevent Toppling Stacks

Folded clothing stacks collapse sideways constantly in closets without dividers. IKEA SKUBB shelf dividers clip onto the shelf and keep stacks organized and upright.

~$8 CAD for a 2-pack at IKEA.

Use them for every folded clothing shelf — sweaters, t-shirts, jeans.

## The Bedroom Storage Priority Order

1. **Storage bed frame** (if buying new) OR **bed risers + SKUBB bags** (if not)
2. **Slim velvet hangers** — immediate closet space gain
3. **Second tension rod** — doubles hanging capacity for short items
4. **Over-door closet organizer** — adds shoes and accessories storage
5. **Floating nightstand shelf** — replaces floor-standing nightstand

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I add storage to a small apartment bedroom?**
Start under the bed — either a storage bed frame or bed risers with flat bins. Then maximize the closet: slim velvet hangers, second tension rod, over-door organizer. Replace a floor-standing nightstand with a floating wall shelf. Use the back of the bedroom door for hooks. Together these changes add more storage than most small bedrooms need.

**What is the best bedroom storage for a Canadian rental apartment?**
A storage bed frame with integrated drawers is the single best investment (IKEA NORDLI from ~$399 CAD). It replaces a full dresser while using no additional floor space. If you cannot replace the bed, bed risers + SKUBB bags (~$39 total) add equivalent storage under an existing frame.

**How do I organize a small bedroom closet on a budget?**
Slim velvet hangers (~$18 CAD) and a second tension rod (~$10 CAD) are the two highest-impact changes. An over-door organizer (~$25 CAD) adds shoe and accessory storage without any installation. SKUBB shelf dividers (~$8 CAD) keep folded stacks organized. Under $65 CAD total for a significantly more organized closet.

**How do I store clothes in a bedroom with no closet?**
IKEA PAX wardrobe columns create a complete closet system with no drilling required. A rolling garment rack with a cover (~$65–$100 CAD total) is a budget alternative. A tall narrow dresser for folded items plus wall-mounted hooks for frequently worn items covers the basics without a dedicated closet.
