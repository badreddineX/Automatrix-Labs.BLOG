The fear of losing your damage deposit holds a lot of renters back from making their apartment feel like home. The result is four blank white walls and furniture from a previous place that does not really work — a space you live in but never really settle into.

Here is what most renters do not know: you can do a lot without touching a wall with a drill. You can create a gallery wall, add shelves, hang curtains, install a feature wall, and completely transform every room — all reversibly.

I have been in my Toronto apartment for three years. My landlord has walked through the unit twice and has never mentioned a single concern. Here is what I have done.

## 1. Command Strips and Hooks — Your Most Important Tool

Command products are the foundation of renter-friendly decorating. They use an adhesive system that bonds firmly to most wall finishes and removes cleanly when pulled straight down — no damage to paint, no holes.

**What Command products can hold:**
- Small Picture Strips: 900 g per pair
- Large Picture Strips: 3.6 kg per pair
- Jumbo Picture Strips: 7.5 kg per pair
- Jumbo Hooks: up to 4.5 kg

Available at Canadian Tire, Walmart Canada, and Amazon.ca. A pack of 4 Jumbo pairs runs ~$15–$20 CAD.

**Important:** follow the removal instructions exactly (pull slowly at a low angle, not straight out from the wall). This is what causes damage when it does happen — people pull incorrectly.

## 2. Peel-and-Stick Wallpaper for a Feature Wall

A peel-and-stick wallpaper panel on the wall behind your sofa or your headboard transforms the entire room — and peels off completely cleanly when you move.

**What to look for:**
- Repositionable adhesive (not permanent)
- Water-based ink (won't bleed into the paint below)
- Self-adhesive removable (not paste-the-wall)

**Canadian options:**
- RoomMates: available on Amazon.ca
- NuWallpaper: Amazon.ca and some Walmart Canada locations
- Tempaper: premium option, Amazon.ca

**Cost:** ~$40–$90 CAD per roll. A typical accent wall takes 2–3 rolls. A full wall in a small apartment bedroom: ~$120–$270 CAD.

## 3. Gallery Wall With Command Strips

A gallery wall above the sofa or along a hallway can be done entirely with Command strips. No drilling required for most art under 3–4 kg per frame.

**How to do it without damage:**
1. Lay all frames on the floor and arrange until satisfied
2. Cut paper templates and tape to the wall with painter's tape to plan the layout
3. Mount each frame with the appropriate Command strip size
4. Remove painter's tape

For heavier frames, use multiple pairs of strips stacked side by side.

**Where to find art in Canada:**
- IKEA PJÄTTERYD series: ~$20–$80 CAD for large prints
- Desenio or Society6: print-on-demand posters
- Your own photos printed at Costco Canada: very affordable for large prints
- Thrift stores: interesting frames for $2–$8 CAD

## 4. Floor-Length Curtains for Every Window

Curtains hung from ceiling to floor change how a room looks and feels. In a rental, the curtain rod should be mounted with the appropriate wall anchor — but most standard curtain hardware can be removed cleanly at move-out.

If you want to be cautious, use a tension rod inside the window frame. IKEA HUGAD or RÄCKA rods work this way for lightweight sheers. For heavier curtain panels, a proper ceiling-mounted or wall-mounted rod is better.

**Cost:**
- IKEA curtain rod: ~$15–$30 CAD
- IKEA LILL sheer panels: ~$10 CAD per pair
- IKEA MERETE heavier panels: ~$40–$60 CAD per pair

## 5. Rugs on Every Floor Surface

If you have hardwood floors, a rug adds warmth and colour. If you have carpet (common in older Canadian buildings), a rug creates a defined zone and covers worn carpet without any installation.

Rugs are portable and move with you. They are the easiest, most impactful floor change available to renters.

**Living room:** 160 × 230 cm minimum
**Bedroom:** rug extending 50–60 cm on three sides of the bed
**Entry:** a welcome mat or small rug 60 × 90 cm

IKEA flat-weave rugs from ~$59 CAD. Wayfair Canada has a wide selection.

## 6. Mirrors Without Drilling

Full-length mirrors can lean against a wall without any wall contact. A leaning mirror from IKEA (HOVET, ~$279 CAD) or a smaller option (~$60–$80 CAD) adds visual depth and reflects light with zero installation.

For wall-mounted round mirrors, Command Jumbo hooks or Command Picture Strips handle most mirrors under 4 kg.

## 7. Temporary Vinyl Tile or Floor Mats for the Kitchen

Some Canadian rental kitchens have cracked or stained vinyl tile that is technically the landlord's responsibility but never gets fixed. Peel-and-stick vinyl tiles go over the existing floor and peel off cleanly — or more practically, just laying large interlocking mats over the floor covers the problem.

**Peel-and-stick vinyl:** ~$2–$4 CAD per tile, available at Amazon.ca and some Canadian hardware stores. Remove before moving out.

**Interlocking foam mats:** ~$25–$60 CAD for a set at Canadian Tire.

## 8. Replace Cabinet Hardware Without Damaging the Original

Cabinet knobs and pulls are held on with a single screw. You can replace every knob in your kitchen and bathroom with upgraded hardware and swap it back before you move out.

This is one of the most dramatic low-cost upgrades available to renters. Old plastic knobs → matte black or brushed brass hardware completely changes the feel of a kitchen.

**Cost:** replacement knobs from ~$3–$8 CAD each at RONA, Home Depot Canada, or Amazon.ca. Save the originals in a bag in a drawer for reinstallation at move-out.

## 9. Plants Throughout the Apartment

Plants require no installation and add more to the feel of an apartment than almost any decor purchase. They add colour, life, texture, and — in dry Canadian winters — a slight improvement to air moisture.

**Easiest to keep alive in rental apartments:**
- Pothos — thrives in any light, almost impossible to kill
- ZZ plant — survives neglect and low light
- Snake plant — very low water, tolerates dim light
- Peace lily — one of the few flowering plants for low light

~$5–$20 CAD per plant at most Canadian garden centres and IKEA.

## 10. Floor Lamps and Table Lamps Instead of Ceiling Fixtures

Rental apartment lighting is usually harsh and institutional. You cannot change the ceiling fixture without permission. But you can add floor lamps, table lamps, and LED strip lights anywhere without touching anything permanent.

Switching from overhead-only to layered lamp lighting changes the feel of a room entirely — warmer, softer, more like a home.

**Cost:** floor lamps from ~$49 CAD at IKEA. Table lamps from ~$30 CAD. LED strip lights ~$20–$35 CAD on Amazon.ca.

Also: swap all bulbs to 2700K warm white. Takes 5 minutes, costs $15–$25 CAD for a multipack, and changes the entire mood of every room.

## 11. Contact Paper for Counters and Shelves

Contact paper — adhesive shelf liner — goes over existing counters, shelves, or cabinet interiors. It peels off cleanly and can completely update the look of a dated kitchen or bathroom.

**Use cases:**
- Dark laminate counters: apply white or marble-effect contact paper
- Inside kitchen cabinets: line with a complementary pattern
- Bathroom shelves: update without painting

**Cost:** ~$10–$20 CAD per roll at Amazon.ca or Canadian Tire

## 12. Fabric Tapestry or Oversized Art for Blank Walls

A large fabric tapestry can be hung from a single Command hook or mounted on a wooden dowel with Command strips. It covers an entire wall section without any damage and adds the same visual impact as wallpaper.

Alternatively, a large-format poster in a clip frame (no glass, lightweight) can be hung with minimal adhesive strips.

**Cost:** fabric tapestries from ~$30–$80 CAD on Amazon.ca. Clip frames from ~$15 CAD at IKEA.

## 13. Upgrade the Bathroom Without Touching Anything Permanent

Rental bathrooms often have dated hardware and minimal storage. You cannot retile or replace the toilet. But you can:

- Add a freestanding over-toilet shelf (~$40–$70 CAD) — no installation
- Replace the toilet seat (~$30–$60 CAD, swap back at move-out)
- Add a new soap dispenser, toothbrush holder, and bathroom tray (~$30–$50 total)
- Hang a small art print with Command strips
- Add plants — pothos or peace lily thrive in bathroom humidity

A bathroom refresh that costs ~$150 CAD and requires zero permanent changes.

## 14. Document Everything Before You Move In

This is not a decor idea — but it protects the entire investment of the ideas above.

On move-in day, photograph every existing crack, stain, scuff, and damage. Send these photos to your landlord in writing (email) and keep a copy for yourself. This is your protection if your landlord tries to charge for pre-existing damage at move-out.

In Ontario, BC, and Quebec, security deposits are regulated. Know your provincial tenant protection rules.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I make my rental apartment look nice without losing my deposit?**
Command strips for art and shelves, peel-and-stick wallpaper for feature walls, leaning mirrors, freestanding storage, rugs on every floor, and floor lamps for lighting. Document the original condition of the apartment with photos before decorating. Remove everything cleanly at move-out and follow Command removal instructions carefully.

**Can I paint my rental apartment in Canada?**
In most cases no, without written permission from your landlord. Under Ontario and BC tenancy law, painting requires landlord consent. Some landlords agree if you return the walls to the original colour before leaving. Always get permission in writing before painting.

**What are the best renter-friendly wall decorations?**
Gallery walls using Command strips, leaning mirrors against the wall, peel-and-stick removable wallpaper, and large fabric tapestries hung from a dowel rod. These add significant wall decoration without any drilling or permanent adhesive.

**Is it safe to use Command strips in a rental apartment?**
Yes, when used correctly. Always follow the removal instructions: pull the tab slowly at a low angle, parallel to the wall. Do not pull straight out from the wall — that is what causes paint to peel. In most rental apartments with standard latex paint, Command strips remove cleanly every time when removed correctly.
