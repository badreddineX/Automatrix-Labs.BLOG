Furniture is the biggest investment in a small apartment, and the biggest risk. The wrong sofa, the wrong storage unit, or the wrong bed frame can eat up your floor space and make a small apartment feel like a storage locker.

I've bought the wrong thing twice in my Toronto apartment. Both times I thought I was making a smart choice. Both times I underestimated how much floor space the piece would consume and how heavy it would feel in the room.

This guide covers the categories that matter most and what to look for in each.

## What to Look for in Small Space Furniture

Before we get into specific picks, three principles:

**1. Apartment-sized dimensions** — full-size furniture is designed for full-size rooms. Look for "apartment" or "compact" versions. A 200 cm sofa in a 3 × 4 m living room leaves no room to walk around it.

**2. Visible legs** — furniture that sits directly on the floor looks heavier. Legs create visual space underneath, making the room feel bigger. This applies to sofas, chairs, beds, and sideboards.

**3. Multi-function** — in a small apartment, every piece should do more than one job. Storage ottoman doubles as a coffee table. Storage bed frame doubles as a dresser. Nesting tables double as a side table or extra surface.

## Sofas for Small Apartments

**IKEA SÖDERHAMN** — $899–$1,299 CAD
The best apartment sofa IKEA makes. Available in 2-seat (151 cm) and 2.5-seat (186 cm) configurations. Modular and expandable. Low-profile with visible legs. The cover is removable and washable.

**Structube NORA 2-seater** — from ~$699 CAD
Clean Scandinavian lines, compact dimensions, available in several fabric colours. Good for a renter who wants something more design-forward than IKEA.

**IKEA FRIHETEN sofa bed** — ~$999 CAD
If you have guests regularly or need a sofa that converts to a bed, FRIHETEN is the best value sofa bed available in Canada. Large hidden storage chaise + sofa + full-size pull-out bed.

**What to avoid:** L-shaped sectionals unless your living room is specifically designed for one. Most small apartments cannot absorb a sectional without losing too much floor space.

## Coffee Tables for Small Living Rooms

**Storage ottoman with tray** — $89–$150 CAD (Wayfair Canada)
This is the best small space coffee table for most renters. Seat extra people, store blankets and magazines inside, add a tray on top for drinks and remotes. Zero sharp corners. Easy to move.

**IKEA LISTERBY round table** — ~$199 CAD
Solid wood, round shape — round tables work better in tight spaces because you don't walk into corners. Compact at 60 cm diameter, fits neatly in front of a 2-seater sofa.

**Nesting tables** — $60–$120 CAD (IKEA or Amazon.ca)
Two or three tables that stack together. Pull the large one out for use, tuck the smaller ones underneath. Takes almost no space when collapsed.

## Beds and Bedroom Furniture

**IKEA NORDLI bed with drawers** — $399–$699 CAD
The best storage bed available in Canada at this price point. Platform bed with 2–4 integrated drawers depending on configuration. White or anthracite finish. Replaces a full dresser. No need for a box spring.

**IKEA HAUGA bed** — from ~$279 CAD
Lower price point, solid wood look, headboard included. No storage, but a clean simple frame with visible legs.

**Bed risers** — ~$25 CAD (Amazon.ca)
If you already have a bed and want to add under-bed clearance for storage bins, bed risers add 15 cm. Instant storage upgrade for $25.

**Dresser vs. storage bed:** In a small bedroom, a storage bed frame holds roughly the same amount as a 3-drawer dresser, while freeing up that floor space entirely. If you're buying new, choose the storage bed.

## Desks for Small Home Offices

**IKEA MICKE** — ~$99 CAD
22 cm depth option works in the narrowest spaces. Cable management built in. Clean white or black finish. This is what I have in my apartment.

**IKEA LINNMON + ADILS** — ~$55 CAD
A large surface at minimal cost. Good if you need horizontal space but not depth. Can be placed against a wall and folded away theoretically (though LINNMON is not wall-mounted).

**Wall-mounted fold-down desk** — $80–$150 CAD (Amazon.ca)
Mounts to the wall, folds flat when not in use. Ideal when you have a dedicated wall but can't commit floor space to a permanent desk.

**Standing desk converter** — $80–$150 CAD
Goes on top of a regular desk or surface and adjusts height. Good if you need flexibility without a full standing desk.

## Storage and Shelving

**IKEA KALLAX** — $79–$259 CAD
The best storage unit for small apartments. Modular, can be oriented horizontally or vertically, works with fabric inserts for concealed storage, DRONA boxes, or doors. Stack two vertically for a tall wardrobe-style unit.

**IKEA BILLY bookcase** — from $79 CAD
Floor-to-ceiling storage for books, display, or with doors added for concealed storage. Inexpensive, well-made, endlessly configurable.

**IKEA PAX wardrobe system** — from $179 CAD per section
If your bedroom has no closet or a terrible one, PAX columns give you a fully configurable wardrobe system. Choose your own internal configuration (drawers, shelves, hanging rods, pull-out units).

**Floating shelves** — $15–$50 CAD per shelf (IKEA LACK)
No floor footprint at all. Use Command strips in rental apartments — good for up to 7.5 kg per pair. Great for books, plants, and display items.

## Dining Options for Small Spaces

**Round dining table** — $150–$350 CAD (IKEA or Wayfair Canada)
Round tables work much better in small spaces than rectangular ones. Two people fit comfortably, and they can be pushed against a wall when not in use to save floor space.

**IKEA NORBERG wall-mounted drop-leaf table** — ~$59 CAD
The ultimate small space dining solution. Folds flat against the wall when not in use. Extends to seat 2. Mounts to the wall with 4 screws.

**Bar stools at the kitchen counter** — add 2 bar stools to a kitchen counter that has an overhang and you have dining seating without a table at all. ~$50–$100 CAD per stool at IKEA or Structube.

## What to Do Before You Buy Anything

1. **Measure your space** — floor plan with dimensions
2. **Measure the furniture** — the actual piece, not the photo
3. **Plan clearance** — 60 cm walkway around all sides, 90 cm in front of the bed
4. **Check delivery access** — can the piece get through your building entrance, elevator, and apartment door?
5. **Think about the exit** — in a rental apartment, you will move eventually. Very large furniture sometimes can't exit the same way it came in

## The Small Space Furniture Priority Order

1. **Fix the sofa first** — the wrong sofa kills the living room
2. **Upgrade the bed** — storage bed frame changes the bedroom completely
3. **Add a storage unit** — KALLAX or BILLY before adding anything else to the room
4. **Coffee table last** — storage ottoman is safest, cheapest, and most flexible

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What furniture is best for a small apartment in Canada?**
Apartment-sized sofa (under 200 cm wide), a storage bed frame (IKEA NORDLI), a KALLAX storage unit, and a compact desk (IKEA MICKE). Prioritize furniture with visible legs and storage built in. IKEA Canada offers the best value for small space furniture, with Structube and Wayfair Canada as alternatives.

**What sofa fits in a small apartment?**
A 2-seat sofa between 150–185 cm wide. Look for apartment-sized options at IKEA (SÖDERHAMN) or Structube (NORA). Avoid L-shaped sectionals unless your room is specifically sized for one. The back height matters too — a lower back makes the room feel more open.

**Is it worth spending more on multi-function furniture?**
Yes, for the most-used pieces. A sofa bed (~$999 CAD) that replaces the need for a guest bed is worth the premium. A storage bed frame (~$499 CAD) that replaces a dresser is worth it. Spending more on a decorative accent table that does nothing except look nice is not.

**Where can I buy small space furniture in Canada?**
IKEA is the best starting point — wide selection, good prices, designed for apartments. Structube for a more design-forward option. Wayfair Canada for variety and frequent sales. Amazon.ca for smaller pieces and accessories. HomeSense for occasional finds at good prices.
