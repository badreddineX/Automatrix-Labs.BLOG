Storage in a small apartment is not about buying more containers. It is about finding the spaces you are not using and making them work.

Most small apartments have more storage capacity than their residents realize — it is just in vertical space, behind doors, and under furniture, rather than in obvious places.

I have reorganized my 510 sq ft Toronto apartment's storage three times. Each time I found more capacity than I thought was there. Here is what I know now.

## Closet Storage Solutions

### 1. Double Your Hanging Space With a Second Rod

A single tension rod hung below the existing closet rod costs $8–$12 CAD at Canadian Tire and instantly doubles the hanging space for shorter items — shirts, jackets, folded pants.

Position the existing rod higher if possible to allow room for the second rod below. This change alone has more impact than any closet organizer system.

### 2. Slim Velvet Hangers Throughout

Standard plastic hangers are 1.5 cm thick. Slim velvet hangers are 0.5 cm. For a closet rod with 60 hangers, that difference frees 60 cm of rod space — almost a full foot of extra hanging space.

~$18 CAD for a 50-pack on Amazon.ca. Takes 20 minutes to switch over. 

### 3. Over-Door Shoe and Accessory Organizer

The inside of the closet door is completely unused in most apartments. An over-door organizer adds 8–24 pockets of storage.

Use the pockets for:
- Shoes (clear-pocket style for visibility)
- Bags and clutches
- Scarves, belts, and accessories
- Small electronics and cables

~$20–$35 CAD at Amazon.ca or Canadian Tire.

### 4. IKEA SKUBB Shelf Dividers and Boxes

Shelf dividers keep folded piles from collapsing sideways. IKEA SKUBB boxes on the top shelf hold items accessed less often — seasonal accessories, formal wear.

SKUBB shelf dividers: ~$8 CAD for a 2-pack. SKUBB boxes: ~$14–$20 CAD per set.

## Bedroom Storage Solutions

### 5. Storage Bed Frame

If you are buying a new bed, get one with storage drawers. IKEA NORDLI with integrated drawers starts at ~$399 CAD and stores the equivalent of a 3-drawer dresser — with no additional floor footprint.

If you already have a bed without storage, proceed to tip 6.

### 6. Bed Risers + Under-Bed Flat Bins

Bed risers ($25 CAD for a set of 4 at Amazon.ca) add ~15 cm of clearance under any existing bed. IKEA SKUBB under-bed bags ($14 CAD for 2) then slide underneath for seasonal storage — extra bedding, off-season clothes, spare towels.

Total: ~$39 CAD for what effectively amounts to a full additional drawer of storage.

### 7. Floating Nightstand Shelf

A floating shelf replaces a floor-standing nightstand with zero floor footprint. IKEA LACK shelf (~$15 CAD) mounted at bedside height holds lamp, phone, water, and a book. Add Command Picture Strips for renter-friendly mounting.

## Kitchen Storage Solutions

### 8. Clear Stackable Pantry Bins

The pantry cabinet becomes dramatically more organized when loose packages are replaced with clear stackable bins. Group by category: pasta, baking supplies, snacks, canned goods.

You can see everything, nothing falls over, and you stop buying duplicates.

~$5–$8 CAD each on Amazon.ca. Six to eight bins transforms a typical apartment pantry.

### 9. Tension Rod Under the Sink

A horizontal tension rod strung across the inside of the under-sink cabinet lets you hang spray bottles by their triggers. This frees up the entire cabinet floor for bins of additional products.

~$8–$10 CAD. One of the highest-impact/lowest-cost storage hacks in any apartment.

### 10. IKEA SKADIS Pegboard for Utensils

Mounted on the wall beside the stove, a pegboard holds spatulas, ladles, tongs, and small pans. Frees up a full drawer and keeps cooking tools immediately accessible.

IKEA SKADIS 56 × 56 cm: ~$25 CAD. Hooks and bins: ~$3–$8 CAD each. Renter note: use Command large picture strips for mounting rather than screws.

![Kitchen wall pegboard with hanging utensils freeing up drawer space](/images/blog-11-img2.jpg)

### 11. Over-Door Pantry Organizer

The inside of the pantry cabinet door (or any kitchen cabinet door) holds an organizer for spices, condiments, foil, and plastic wrap. Items that cluttered cabinet shelves get their own dedicated home on the door.

~$15–$25 CAD at Amazon.ca for an adjustable organizer.

### 12. Vertical Magazine Files for Flat Items

Cutting boards, baking sheets, and pot lids stored horizontally mean pulling the whole stack out to get the bottom one. A vertical file organizer (~$8–$12 CAD at IKEA, FLUNS series) stores these items vertically so each is individually accessible.

## Bathroom Storage Solutions

### 13. Freestanding Over-Toilet Shelf

Uses the blank wall above the toilet — the most underutilized bathroom real estate. Adds 2–3 shelves with no installation required.

~$40–$70 CAD at Amazon.ca or Canadian Tire. Holds toilet paper, towels, toiletries, and a small plant.

### 14. Shower Caddy and Corner Shelf

A tension-pole shower caddy requires no drilling and adds multiple shelves inside the shower. A corner shelf for outside the shower holds daily skincare products.

Shower caddy: ~$25–$45 CAD. Corner shelf: ~$20–$35 CAD.

## Living Room Storage Solutions

### 15. IKEA KALLAX With Fabric Inserts

The KALLAX storage unit is the workhorse of small apartment living rooms. Open cubes work for display. Add DRONA or KUGGIS fabric inserts for concealed storage — blankets, electronics, charging cables, whatever you want accessible but out of sight.

KALLAX 2×4 unit: ~$169 CAD. Fabric inserts: ~$6 CAD each at IKEA.

### 16. Storage Ottoman as a Coffee Table

Replaces the coffee table with a storage unit that also serves as additional seating, a foot rest, and a display surface (with a tray). Inside holds blankets, board games, remote controls, charging cables — whatever you want in the living room but off every surface.

~$89–$150 CAD at Wayfair Canada or Amazon.ca.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What are the best storage solutions for a small apartment in Canada?**
Slim velvet hangers (closet), under-bed bins (bedroom), clear stackable pantry bins (kitchen), over-toilet freestanding shelf (bathroom), and storage ottoman (living room). These five changes address the most common storage problems in Canadian rental apartments for under $200 CAD total.

**How do I add storage to a small apartment without drilling?**
Command hooks and strips for wall-mounted items. Tension rods under the sink and in the closet. Freestanding units like over-toilet shelves and over-door organizers. Bed risers for under-bed clearance. KALLAX units with fabric inserts for living room storage. None of these require drilling.

**How do I maximize storage in a rental apartment?**
Think vertical first — most renters waste wall space above furniture. Think doors second — inside of closet doors, cabinet doors, and bathroom doors are all unused storage. Think under furniture third — especially under the bed. These three areas combined add more storage than most renters realize they have.

**What is the best under-bed storage for a small apartment?**
IKEA SKUBB under-bed bags (~$14 CAD for 2) paired with bed risers (~$25 CAD) if needed. For permanent storage, a bed frame with integrated drawers like IKEA NORDLI is the best long-term solution. Flat rigid bins with lids from Amazon.ca are good for items you need to access seasonally.
