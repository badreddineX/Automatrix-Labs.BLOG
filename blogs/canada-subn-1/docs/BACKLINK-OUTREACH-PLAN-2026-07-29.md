# Canada (smallspacehome.ca) — Backlink Outreach Plan

Written 2026-07-29. Context: both Canada and UK are holding at GSC average position ~40 despite
solid on-page SEO (confirmed by `SEO-AUDIT-2026-07-25.md` and `SEO-AUDIT-2026-07-26.md` — titles,
meta descriptions, headings, and internal linking are all already in good shape). At 2 weeks old,
this is a domain-trust/backlink problem, not an on-page problem — Google doesn't rank a brand-new
domain highly for competitive terms regardless of content quality until external sites vouch for
it. This plan is the one concrete lever left that hasn't been tried yet.

## Why this, why now

- Long-tail, low-competition queries (e.g. the bathroom-storage query at pos ~4.6-4.9) are already
  ranking well — proof the content and on-page work are fine.
- Head terms ("small space furniture," "small apartment storage ideas") sit at pos 20-40+ — proof
  it's a competition/authority gap, not a content gap.
- No backlink-building activity has been logged anywhere in this repo so far. This is a genuine
  unaddressed gap, unlike on-page SEO which has already been audited twice.

## Pitch angle

Lead with your strongest, most evidence-backed content — the bathroom storage / rental-storage
angle, since it's independently confirmed by both GSC position and Pinterest engagement as your
best material (see `../CANADA-ANALYTICS.md`). A pitch built around "storage solutions for renters
who can't drill or renovate" is specific enough to stand out from generic decor pitches these sites
likely get in volume.

## Process

- **Personalize each pitch** — don't mass-send identical copies; reference something specific
  about the target site.
- **Track outcomes** in a simple log (site, date pitched, response, published Y/N, link acquired)
  so this can be evaluated the same evidence-based way as everything else in this repo.
- **Pace it** — 2-3 pitches a week is plenty; this is a slow-compounding lever, not a traffic
  spike source.

## What NOT to do

- **Don't buy links or use link farms/PBNs** — high risk of a Google penalty that would set the
  site back much further than the current position-40 plateau.
- **Don't mass-submit to low-quality directories** — thin, irrelevant backlinks provide little to
  no ranking benefit and occasionally correlate with worse trust signals.
- **Don't expect fast results** — backlinks compound slowly, typically weeks to months before a
  ranking effect is visible. This doesn't replace or shorten the 2026-08-24 hold on other SEO
  changes; it's additive, not a shortcut.
