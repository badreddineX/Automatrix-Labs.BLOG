# SmallSpace Home (CAD) — SEO Audit

**Date:** 2026-07-25
**Scope:** Full site — technical SEO, on-page SEO, schema, content/E-E-A-T, images
**Method:** Direct source review (Astro repo) + live production checks (smallspacehome.ca) + cross-reference with `CANADA-ANALYTICS.md` GSC data
**Status:** User explicitly requested fixing all issues now (overriding the 2026-07-24 hold for this pass). Fixes applied 2026-07-25 — see "Fix log" at the bottom for what changed, what was intentionally skipped, and one correction to this report.

---

## Site health snapshot

Overall this is a well-built, technically clean site for its age (22 posts, live since 2026-07-16). No critical/blocking issues found. robots.txt, sitemap, HTTPS, security headers, canonical tags, and per-post schema are all correctly implemented. The issues below are optimization gaps, not fires.

---

## HIGH priority

### 1. `/blog` archive page has a generic, keyword-empty title tag
Live title is just **"Blog | SmallSpace Home"** (`src/pages/blog/index.astro:35`). No mention of "apartment," "organization," "decor," or "Canada." As the post count grows, this page will accumulate the most internal links and is a natural candidate to rank for broad terms — right now its title gives Google nothing to match against.
**Fix:** e.g. `title="Apartment Organization & Decor Ideas"` (SEO.astro appends " | SmallSpace Home" automatically).

### 2. Homepage ranks worse than several inner posts despite the most impressions
Per `CANADA-ANALYTICS.md` (2026-07-21 pull): homepage sits at ~pos 47-66 with 32 impressions (most on the site), while `ikea-small-space-hacks` sits at ~pos 3 with far fewer impressions. The homepage H1 ("Small Space Living, Done Beautifully") is a branded tagline, not phrasing that matches how people actually search ("small apartment organization," "apartment decor ideas"). Title tag is decent, but H1 + intro copy could reinforce the head terms more directly.
**Fix:** Keep the tagline as a supporting line, but make sure the H1 or the immediately-following copy contains the actual target phrase(s). Also worth strengthening internal links *to* the homepage from posts (currently posts link to `/blog` and each other, rarely to `/`).

### 3. No sitewide Organization/WebSite schema, no BreadcrumbList schema
Every post page has a real breadcrumb (`Home › Blog › Category`) rendered in HTML, but there's no matching `BreadcrumbList` JSON-LD — an easy, low-effort rich-result opportunity being left on the table. There's also no sitewide `Organization`/`WebSite` JSON-LD (helps Google understand the brand entity; supports sitelinks search box eligibility). Currently `SEO.astro` only emits `BlogPosting` and `FAQPage` schema on article pages.
**Fix:** Add `BreadcrumbList` JSON-LD to `BlogPost.astro` (breadcrumb markup already exists — just needs the JSON-LD twin), and one sitewide `Organization`/`WebSite` block (could live in a base layout or the homepage).

### 4. Homepage `<title>` is also over the truncation point
Rendered live title is **"Organization & Decor for Canadian Apartments | SmallSpace Home"** — 64 characters, past the ~60-char point where Google typically truncates. Confirmed on the live page (`curl` check), not just the source. Related to #2 but a distinct, purely mechanical fix.
**Fix:** Shorten the descriptive part by ~5-8 characters, e.g. "Decor & Organization for Canadian Apartments".

### 5. `/404` page is `noindex`ed but not excluded from the sitemap
`404.astro` correctly sets `<meta name="robots" content="noindex" />`, but `astro.config.mjs`'s sitemap filter only excludes `/thank-you` (`filter: (page) => !page.includes('/thank-you')`). If Astro's sitemap integration picks up the static `/404` route, it ends up listed in `sitemap-index.xml` while simultaneously telling Google not to index it — a small, self-contradicting signal. Worth verifying against the live `sitemap-index.xml` and adding `/404` to the filter if it's present.

## MEDIUM priority

### 6. Several post titles sit right at/over Google's truncation point
With the " | SmallSpace Home" suffix (19 chars) added by `SEO.astro`, these run 60-62 characters total — right where Google starts truncating (~575px, roughly 60 chars):
- "Apartment Decor Ideas for Canadian Renters" (62)
- "Bedroom Storage Ideas for Small Apartments" (62)
- "14 Small Space Decorating Rules That Work" (62)
- "14 Small Space Kitchen Organization Ideas" (61)

Not urgent — the brand suffix is what gets cut, not the meaningful part — but worth trimming a few characters on the next content pass so the full brand name survives in the SERP.

### 7. Thin author entity (E-E-A-T gap)
Every post shows the same one-line author blurb + initials avatar ("BB") linking to `/about`. There's no dedicated author page with credentials, no `sameAs` links (social profiles, etc.) in the `Person` schema on `BlogPosting`. The actual first-hand content (real CAD prices, a genuinely lived-in 510 sq ft Toronto rental) is a strong authenticity signal — the schema/author-page plumbing just isn't reinforcing it. This matters more post-December-2025-core-update, which weighted E-E-A-T signals up.
**Fix:** Expand `/about` into a fuller author bio (background, why-trust-this-content), and add `sameAs` to the `Person` object in `SEO.astro`'s `articleJsonLd` once there's a social profile to point to.

### 8. `/blog` and `/about` H1s don't carry primary keywords
- `/blog` H1: "Read All Organization & Decor" — acceptable but generic.
- `/about` H1: "It's not just a rental. It's our home." — fine for brand voice, but carries zero keyword weight (lower priority than #1/#2 since About isn't a target landing page).

### 9. Known keyword cannibalization — already identified, decision already logged
Not a new finding — flagging so it's not lost from "all issues." `CANADA-ANALYTICS.md`'s 2026-07-21 entry already identified that 5 posts are near-duplicate general decor content and 2 are near-duplicate general organization content, competing with each other for the same search intent. Standing decision: leave the published posts live, stop writing new posts in those two lanes unless genuinely differentiated. No new action needed here — just surfacing it as a live SEO issue, not just a historical note.

### 10. No shared base `Layout.astro` — every page hand-rolls its own `<head>`
`index.astro`, `about.astro`, `blog/index.astro`, `404.astro`, `terms.astro`, `privacy-policy.astro`, and `thank-you.astro` each independently declare `<html lang="en-CA">`, `<meta charset>`, viewport, favicon, and the `<SEO>` call — there's no single base layout wrapping them. Functionally fine today (all current pages are consistent), but it's a structural risk: a future new page can easily forget a meta tag or the favicon link with nothing to catch it. Not itself an SEO defect, but the mechanism by which future SEO defects are most likely to be introduced.

## LOW priority / nice-to-haves

### 11. No `llms.txt`
Not required, but a lightweight AI-crawler guidance file is a cheap GEO/AI-citation signal now that ChatGPT/Perplexity/AI Overviews are a real discovery channel. Low urgency given the site's current age/authority.

### 12. No Content-Security-Policy header
HSTS, X-Frame-Options, X-Content-Type-Options, Permissions-Policy, Referrer-Policy are all present (Vercel defaults + good config). CSP is missing — a security hardening item, not a ranking factor, but worth a mention.

### 13. Static pages all share one default OG image
`/about`, `/terms`, `/privacy-policy` all fall back to `/images/about-hero-apartment.jpg` for social previews. Low priority since these pages are rarely shared directly.

### 14. No `twitter:site` / `twitter:creator` meta tag
`SEO.astro` sets `twitter:card`, `twitter:title`, `twitter:description`, `twitter:image` — but not `twitter:site` (the brand's X/Twitter handle). Cosmetic; only matters once there's an active X account to attribute.

### 15. `gtag.js` is initialized inline on every single page load
`SEO.astro` (included on every page) re-declares `window.dataLayer` and calls `gtag('js', ...)` / `gtag('config', ...)` per page — the standard GA4 snippet pattern, so functionally correct, just noting it's duplicated logic rather than centralized once. Not a bug, not worth touching on its own.

## What's already working well

- **Per-post schema is solid:** every article emits `BlogPosting` + `FAQPage` JSON-LD, and the FAQ content directly answers real queries seen in GSC (e.g. the bathroom-storage FAQs match the "where can i buy bathroom storage..." query almost verbatim).
- **Meta descriptions** are consistently well-written, keyword-rich, and land in the ~150-160 char sweet spot across all 22 posts.
- **Canonical tags are correct even under the `/blog?category=X` client-side filters** — they all self-canonicalize to bare `/blog`, so the category/pagination JS UI creates no duplicate-content risk.
- **Internal linking** is genuinely good: algorithmic related-posts (category + tag scored), a "Continue reading" module, and real contextual in-body links (e.g. bathroom-storage → bedroom-storage, → organization-on-a-budget).
- **Images** are already reasonably optimized (mostly 50-300KB), all have explicit `width`/`height` (good for CLS), and `loading="eager"` is scoped correctly to above-the-fold images only.
- **Redirect hygiene:** the renamed `storage-solutions-for-small-apartments` → `storage-ideas-for-small-places` is handled via a proper 301 in `astro.config.mjs`, avoiding an orphaned URL.
- **Thank-you page** is correctly `noindex`ed and excluded from the sitemap.
- **Production matches source** — spot-checked the live homepage's title/meta/canonical/og:image against the repo; no drift, no deploy issues.
- Fast, small pages (37KB homepage HTML, <1s response) and full security header set via Vercel.

## Audit scope note

This was a manual source + live-site review (repo files + targeted `curl`/rendered-HTML checks against smallspacehome.ca), not a full automated crawl — there's no crawler tool available in this environment. Every page type (home, blog index, post, about, 404, terms, privacy, thank-you) and all 22 post files were individually checked, but a handful of edge cases weren't independently verified beyond source inspection: whether `/404` actually appears in the live `sitemap-index.xml` (#5), and whether any orphaned/broken internal links exist beyond the ones read in content. Flagging so "all issues" doesn't imply a guarantee of zero unknown-unknowns.
