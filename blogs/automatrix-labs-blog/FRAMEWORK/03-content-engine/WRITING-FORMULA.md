# STEP 03 — Content Engine
> The exact formula for writing blog posts that rank AND convert.

---

## THE WINNING POST STRUCTURE

```
[TITLE] — include keyword, add a number or year, make it specific
    ↓
[INTRO] — hook (problem) → promise (what they'll get) → credibility
    ↓
[BODY] — H2 sections covering the topic fully
    ↓
[FAQ] — 3-5 questions from "People Also Ask"
    ↓
[CONCLUSION] — summary + CTA (affiliate link / email signup)
```

## WRITING RULES

### DO:
- ✅ Write for humans first, Google second
- ✅ Use short paragraphs (2-3 lines max)
- ✅ Add real examples, numbers, and data
- ✅ Include your keyword in: title, first 100 words, one H2, conclusion
- ✅ Use bullet points and tables — easier to scan
- ✅ Link to 2-3 of your other posts (internal links)
- ✅ Link to 1 authoritative external source
- ✅ Aim for 1,500-2,500 words for most posts
- ✅ Aim for 3,000+ words for pillar posts

### DON'T:
- ❌ Keyword stuff (repeating keyword unnaturally)
- ❌ Write thin content under 800 words
- ❌ Copy competitor content
- ❌ Publish without filling the SEO checklist first
- ❌ Leave posts without a CTA

## INTRO FORMULA (the most important part)

**Hook options:**
- Start with a surprising stat: "83% of bloggers never make $100..."
- Start with a question: "What if you could rank on Google without backlinks?"
- Start with a pain: "You've been writing for months and still getting zero traffic..."

**Then:**
- Acknowledge the problem they're facing
- Promise what they'll get from this post
- Tell them what's covered (table of contents for long posts)
