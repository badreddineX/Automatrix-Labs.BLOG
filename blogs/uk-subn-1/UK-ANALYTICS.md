## Scaling Decisions Log

Running log of decisions made based on the data above (e.g. "doubling down on X topic cluster", "killing Y pin format", "adding store link to Z article").

- **2026-08-07 — Fixed hub/spoke keyword cannibalization on `kitchen-on-a-budget-uk.md`,** the site's strongest page by far (1,269 impr/28d, 5x the #2 page) but fragmented across 210 distinct queries — the most extreme case found across any of the three blogs. Root cause: 3 of its 14 sections (splashback, worktop, flooring) fully duplicated the depth of their own dedicated spoke articles linked at the bottom of the same post, so Google saw two full-depth pages competing for the same terms. Trimmed those 3 sections to a short summary + link out to the spoke page, consolidating ranking signal instead of splitting it. Commit `01785ad`. **Next check:** does query fragmentation narrow and do the spoke pages (worktop/splashback/flooring) start picking up impressions of their own in the next GSC pull.

- **2026-08-03 — Built 10 new pins for the 10 UK articles that had zero Pinterest coverage.** Gap-analysis of `pin-generator/pins.json` against all 38 published UK articles found 10 (all kitchen/storage-focused: bathroom-storage, budget-kitchen-flooring, budget-kitchen-splashback, earthy-neutral-kitchen-colours, hidden-pantry, rental-kitchen-upgrade, small-bedroom-storage, small-kitchen-island, small-kitchen-storage-budget, vertical-storage-flats) with no pin at all. Used the newly-installed `hormozi-hooks` skill ([[project_blogging_ressources_hub]]) to write headlines applying the WHO+RESULT+OBJECTION-REMOVAL formula, leaning into the renter/deposit-safe framing that's this project's proven differentiator (see the bathroom-storage GSC win in `CANADA-ANALYTICS.md`) rather than the generic style-category titles most existing pins use (Cottagecore, Maximalist, etc.). Reused each article's existing cover image — no new photography.
