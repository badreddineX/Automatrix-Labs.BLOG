## Pitch angle

Lead with the kitchen-on-a-budget angle — it's your largest, best-established topic cluster by a
wide margin (48% of all impressions) and the strongest evidence of what the site does well. A
pitch specifically about budget kitchen updates for UK renters/homeowners is more likely to land
than a generic "we write about home decor" pitch.

### Draft pitch email (adapt per site, don't send verbatim/identical copies)

> Subject: Guest post pitch — budget kitchen updates for UK homes
>
> Hi [name],
>
> I run britishhomeinterior.co.uk, focused on affordable home updates for UK homes and rentals —

>

>
> Thanks,
> [name] / britishhomeinterior.co.uk

## Process

- **Personalize each pitch**, track outcomes in a simple log (site, date, response, published Y/N,
  link acquired) — same discipline as Canada's plan, so results are comparable and evidence-based.
- **Pace at 2-3 pitches/week.** This is a slow lever, not a quick fix.
- Since UK is ~1 week behind Canada on nearly every other metric, expect backlink outreach results
  to lag Canada's by a similar margin — don't read a slower response rate here as UK-specific
  failure without comparing timing first.

## What NOT to do

- **Don't buy links, use link farms, or mass-submit to low-quality directories** — real risk of a
  Google penalty, which would be far worse than the current plateau.
- **Don't expect this to shortcut the 2026-08-24 hold** on other SEO/content changes — this is
  additive, and backlink effects take weeks to months to show up in position data.
