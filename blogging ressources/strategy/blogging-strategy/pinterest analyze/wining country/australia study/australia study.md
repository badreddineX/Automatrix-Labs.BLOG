# Australia — Pinterest Country Study

## User Base
- **Total Pinterest users**: 5.68 million (late 2025)
- **% of total population reached**: 21.0%
- **% of Australian consumers on Pinterest**: 32%
- **User growth**: +355,000 users (+6.7%) between July–October 2025
- Tier 2 monetization market — strong value, lower competition than US

---

## Demographics

### Gender
- Female: **~70%+** (higher female skew than global average)
- Largest female segments:
  - **25–34**: 921,000 users
  - **18–24**: 807,000 users

### Age Groups
- Core audience: **18–34** — young adult female majority
- Older audience (35–54) also present and growing
- Household decision-makers heavily represented — strong purchase intent

---

## User Behavior
- Content has a **long tail** — evergreen pins stay discoverable for months
- Visual search keeps older pins surfacing in search results
- Users skew toward household spending decisions — home, lifestyle, beauty
- Strong engagement in fashion, home decor, and food categories

## Targeting Australia on Pinterest

### Language
- Mix of **UK and US English** — lean toward US English for Pinterest (algorithm is US-oriented)
- Avoid purely UK terms — Australian audience accepts American vocabulary on Pinterest
- Australian-specific terms to include where relevant: backyard (not garden), arvo, etc. — use sparingly in descriptions, not board titles

### Peak Pinning Hours (AEST — Australian Eastern Standard Time)
- Weekdays: **9PM – 11PM AEST**
- Weekends: **3PM – 9PM AEST**
- Note: Australia is **10–16 hours ahead** of the US — schedule separately from US content

## Why Australia Is a Winning Country

- English-speaking — same content strategy as US with minor adjustments
- 32% consumer penetration — high reach relative to population
- Strong purchasing power — high CPM rates from Australian advertisers
- Less competition than the US — easier to rank organically
- Growing platform — +6.7% user growth in just 3 months (2025)
- Climate opposite to US — Christmas content in summer = unique content angle

---

## Australia vs US Content Timing Note
Australia's summer = December–February (when the US is in winter).
This means you can create **summer-themed Christmas content** that stands out from the typical "cozy winter Christmas" content flooding the US market.
