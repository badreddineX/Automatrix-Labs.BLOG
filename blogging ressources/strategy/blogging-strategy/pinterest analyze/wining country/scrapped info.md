## Countries by Traffic Value (Monetization Tiers)

### Tier 1 — Highest Value (Best CPM + Affiliate Commissions)
- **United States** — Largest advertiser budgets, highest affiliate commissions, best niches: home & DIY, food, personal finance, health & wellness
- **Canada** — Near US-level purchasing power, similar consumer behavior
- **United Kingdom** — Europe's strongest market, mature ecommerce, solid affiliate networks

### Tier 2 — High Value (Strong ROI with right niche)
- **Germany**
- **France**
- **Netherlands**
- **Australia & New Zealand**
- **Nordic countries** (Sweden, Norway, Denmark, Finland)

### Tier 3 — Moderate Value
- **Eastern Europe** (Poland, Romania, Czechia)
- **Latin America** (Brazil, Mexico, Argentina, Colombia) — huge user base but lower CPM
- **Japan**

### Tier 4 — Lower Value
- India, Southeast Asia, Africa — growth potential but low per-visitor earnings

---

## Key Insight
Pinterest does not pay creators by region. Money is made **after the click** through:
- Blog ad revenue (CPM from ad networks)
- Affiliate marketing
- Digital products
- Brand sponsorships

**Targeting Tier 1 countries = higher earnings per visitor regardless of your location.**
