## Keyword Strategy for Australia

- Use **US English spelling** as the base — Australian Pinterest audience responds to American vocabulary on the platform
- Add locally relevant terms where natural: "backyard" (not garden), "Australia," "Aussie" in descriptions — not board titles
- Target **low competition long-tail keywords first** — AU market is smaller than US, easier to rank
- The **opposite season angle** is your strongest unique weapon — create summer content when the rest of the world posts winter content (Christmas BBQ, summer entertaining, beach road trips in December)
- Use Pinterest Trends filtered to **Australia** to validate keyword demand
