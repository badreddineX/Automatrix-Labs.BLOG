## Full description

**What it is:** A complete 15-page apartment reset system you run twice a year — a deep January reset and a lighter spring follow-up — covering the bedroom, closet, kitchen, pantry, living room, bathroom, and entryway. Built specifically for renters and small-space living in Canada.

**Why twice a year:** One-time "declutter your home" checklists get used once and lost. This one is designed to come back. The built-in Reset Log tracks every January and spring from 2026 to 2028, so instead of starting from zero each time, you're maintaining a system that already works.

**It's fillable.** Every checklist box ticks on-screen in any PDF reader (Acrobat, Edge, Chrome, Preview), and the Reset Log has type-in fields for your dates and notes. Prefer paper? Print it — it's designed to work both ways.

**Real shopping lists, real prices.** Every room ends with a short "what to buy" list using actual Canadian retailers — IKEA Canada, Amazon.ca, Wayfair Canada — with honest price ranges (the full list totals under $160 CAD, and most apartments only need 2–3 items). Plus a 6-item IKEA Essentials Blueprint for anyone furnishing a small space from scratch.
