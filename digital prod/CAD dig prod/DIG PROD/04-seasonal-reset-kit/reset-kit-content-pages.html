January Reset

Bedroom & Closet

Declutter the closet completely

  • Pull everything out — every item
  • Keep only clothing worn in the past 12 months
  • Holiday gift clothing: try it on now, decide now
  • Shoes not worn in the past year → donate
  • Keep one spare bedding set — no more

Reorganize what stays

  • Switch to slim velvet hangers (frees 30–40% of rod space)
  • Hang by category: tops, bottoms, dresses, outerwear
  • Use shelf dividers so folded stacks stop collapsing
  • Reassess under-bed storage — compress seasonal items flat

January Reset

Kitchen & Pantry

Pantry and food storage

  • Empty the pantry completely
  • Check every expiry date — holiday baking, canned goods from November
  • Consolidate duplicates (three open bags of pasta → one)
  • Donate non-expired items you genuinely won't use

Organize what stays

Clear stackable bins by category work far better than loose items on shelves. Label each bin: grains, canned goods, snacks, baking, breakfast — items behind other items get forgotten, bins solve this.

Kitchen tools, appliances & the under-sink cabinet

  • Ask of every rarely-used appliance: does this earn its cabinet space?
  • Empty the under-sink cabinet completely
  • Install a tension rod across the middle to hang spray bottles
  • Use a turntable for products at the back

January Reset

Living Room, Bathroom & Entryway

Living area

  • Box holiday decor by category (lights, ornaments, textiles) and label clearly
  • Clear every surface completely — only intentional items go back
  • Empty the storage ottoman or media console and reassess what's inside

Bathroom

  • Check expiry dates on skincare, haircare, and medication
  • Rule for duplicates: finish one before opening the next
  • Counter holds daily-use items only — everything else in a drawer
  • Empty and re-group the under-sink cabinet by category

Entryway

  • One hook per household member for daily-use outerwear
  • One designated spot for daily footwear (boot tray if wet)
  • One spot for keys, transit cards, daily-carry items
  • Move seasonal/rarely-used items to the closet or under-bed

The three rules that make a reset actually hold

1. Declutter before you organize. Organizing around clutter is why systems fail — remove the excess first.
2. Every item needs a home. If something doesn't have a specific place, it ends up on a surface.
3. Systems have to fit how you actually live. The best system is the one you can keep up in 5 minutes a day.

Spring Reset

Bedroom & Seasonal Swap

Declutter

  • Pull everything out of the closet — everything
  • The changing-room test: would you buy this again right now? If not, it leaves
  • Shoes not worn since last spring → donate
  • Empty and reassess under-bed storage

The winter → summer seasonal swap

Winter coats, heavy sweaters, snow pants, and wool items go into vacuum storage bags → under-bed or a high shelf. Spring/summer clothing moves to the front of the closet and accessible drawers. Vacuum bags compress winter clothing to a fraction of its size.

Deep clean

  • Wash all bedding including duvet cover and pillowcases
  • Flip or rotate the mattress
  • Clean under the bed — not just where you can see
  • Dust all surfaces including furniture tops and window sills

CONTENT PAGE 6 — Spring Reset · Kitchen
Spring Reset

Kitchen

Declutter

  • Remove everything from every cabinet and the pantry
  • Check expiry dates on everything — expired goods out
  • Duplicate tools: keep one, donate the rest
  • Appliances unused in 12 months → donate
  • Spices older than 12–18 months → replace or remove

Pantry rotation

Move older items to the front, newer items to the back — this prevents the "found a tin of tomatoes from three years ago" problem. Add clear stackable bins by category; spring is the ideal time to install a proper pantry system you'll maintain all year.

Deep clean

  • Wipe down every cabinet surface inside and out
  • Clean the refrigerator — remove all items, clean shelves, check condiments
  • Clean the oven (often the most neglected appliance in winter)
  • Wipe down appliance exteriors and behind anything movable

CONTENT PAGE 7 — Spring Reset · Living Room, Bathroom & Entryway
Spring Reset

Living Room, Bathroom & Entryway

Living room

  • Remove winter-specific textiles (heavy throws, seasonal cushions) → wash, fold, store
  • Box and label leftover seasonal decor for next year
  • Vacuum under and behind all furniture; clean windows
  • Re-style shelves — add spring/summer textiles once winter ones are stored

Bathroom

  • Check expiry on every product — sunscreen, medication, makeup
  • Scrub the tile grout, clean the medicine cabinet, wash the bath mat
  • Organize with drawer dividers and categorized under-sink bins

Entryway

  • Clean, dry, and store all winter footwear and outerwear
  • Deep clean the floor — winter roads bring in salt residue
  • Reorganize hooks for spring outerwear (lighter coats, rain jackets)
